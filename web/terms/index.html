Cairn Pro and Teams orders

A quote is an estimate of expected work. Vanity mining is a random search: a job can finish much sooner or much later than its estimate. The price covers the expected work, not a guaranteed completion time. We start mining once payment is detected on-chain and continue until a result is found or you request a refund under the policy below.

Refunds

If a job has run past five times its estimated time without a result, you may request a full refund from the order page. Refunds are sent to the address you provide, on the chain you paid on, within two business days. Jobs completed before a refund is processed are delivered and the refund is void. Unpaid orders expire after 24 hours and incur no charge. Payments sent to the wrong chain, in the wrong token, or after expiry are not detected automatically; contact us with the transaction hash and we will do our best to recover them, less network fees.

What we deliver

For Pro orders, an offset which, combined with your private half, yields a key controlling an address matching your target. For Teams jobs, a CREATE2 salt. We verify every result independently before releasing it. We do not deploy contracts or fund addresses for you.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to impersonate another party's address or to facilitate fraud. Address-poisoning patterns that mimic a specific victim's address will be refused and refunded.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.
